Attempt basis, stated plainly.

Repairs are conducted on an "attempt" basis. Edge Computing will attempt the agreed-upon repairs to the best of their ability. However, some manufacturers put safeguards in place to try to prevent their devices from being serviced by third parties, which can, in rare cases, lead to further damage when repairs are attempted. Edge Computing will not be liable for any damages caused during attempted repairs. In the event that we are unable to conduct the agreed-upon repairs, charges for that repair will not be applied.
